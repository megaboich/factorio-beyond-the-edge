---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Added the Stargate window: a name (random at first), the paired gate, an on/off switch, and a circuit enable condition.
    - Each gate runs on a 10 GJ power bank (see docs/power.md), charged from spare network power at up to 1 GW and leaking 0.5 % of its charge a second (50 MW when full). An enabled gate opens once it is full, spending 8 GJ, then runs its 1 GW upkeep from the store while the network refills it at up to 200 MW more, shows a rippling veil, and closes when the store runs dry. Its status lights turn red while it is short of power.
    - A "Keep charged when deactivated" option keeps a switched-off gate charged.
    - A gate with no partner sends travellers to the unexplored area closest to the landing site of a random visited planet.
    - Sounds for the gate opening and closing, and for travellers leaving and arriving.
    - Walking into it takes you, after two seconds in the ripples, out of the front of its partner. Only the gate's machinery is walled off: you can walk through a closed gate and around behind it, and an open one sends you from either side. Cars, tanks, spidertron legs, and ground enemies are kept out of the machinery too.
    - Cars, tanks, and spidertrons travel through gates, crewed, empty, or remotely driven, keeping crew, fuel, cargo, and equipment, and drive or walk out of the partner; a gate refuses a vehicle its destination's surface cannot hold. Biters, spitters, and pentapods that wander in travel too. A remotely driven tank's or spidertron's driver is put out of it as it enters, the view staying behind. Arrivals are not sent straight back through an open partner.
    - Train station mode: a gate can lay one track used both ways, or two tracks with departures on the right or the left, with a station named after the gate. Trains whose front reaches the gate's edge travel carriage by carriage to the paired station gate, keeping cargo, fluid, fuel, driver, schedule, interrupts, and train group, and continue past the gate's station. Clicking a station gate opens its train stop window with the gate's window beside it. Station gates show track on a ramp into the ring, and each carriage passes the veil with its own splash and sound.
    - The gate's power bank is visible: capacitor banks with charge lights along its west and east walls.
    - Added the Stargate construction kit, which holds the scarce high-end parts of a gate and some structural material.
    - Added the 14x14 Stargate construction site, which takes exactly the bulk materials, a whole number of stacks each.
    - Added the Stargate, built over a minute in six phases with a progress bar, starting from the construction site's own look. It can only be destroyed.
    - Added the technology progression: Experimental gates (kit and unpaired random jumps), Controllable Stargate jumps (gate pairing, researchable once a character has travelled through an unpaired gate), and Stargate rail transit (train station mode).
    - On Aquilo a gate freezes unless heat pipes keep it warm (1 MW): frozen, it cannot charge or open, but still receives. The construction site and the gate under construction keep working in the cold and show frozen pictures while unheated.
    - Unpaired random destinations now verify collision-free solid ground for the traveller or vehicle, skipping chunks covered by water or lava.
    - The gate is a modern steel ring between two support towers, standing in a concrete basin with a trench that keeps the whole ring visible.
