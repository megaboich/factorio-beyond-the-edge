---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Added the Stargate window: a name (random at first), the paired gate, an on/off switch, and a circuit enable condition.
    - Each gate runs on a 10 GJ power bank (see docs/power.md), charged from spare network power at up to 1 GW with 95 % efficiency and leaking 0.5 % of its charge a second (50 MW when full). An enabled gate opens once it is full, spending 8 GJ, then runs its 1 GW upkeep from the store while the network refills it at up to 200 MW more, shows a rippling veil, and closes when the store runs dry. Its status lights turn red while it is short of power.
    - A "Keep charged when deactivated" option keeps a switched-off gate charged.
    - A gate with no partner sends travellers to the unexplored area closest to the landing site of a random visited planet.
    - Sounds for the gate opening and closing, and for travellers leaving and arriving.
    - Walking into it takes you, after two seconds in the ripples, out of the front of its partner. Only the gate's machinery is walled off: you can walk through a closed gate and around behind it, and an open one sends you from either side. Vehicles pass through the walls.
    - The gate's power bank is visible: capacitor banks with charge lights along its west and east walls.
    - Added the Stargate construction kit, which holds the scarce high-end parts of a gate and some structural material.
    - Added the 14x14 Stargate construction site, which takes exactly the bulk materials, a whole number of stacks each.
    - Added the Stargate, built over a minute in three phases with a progress bar, starting from the construction site's own look. It can only be destroyed.
    - Added the Experimental gates technology.
    - The gate is a modern steel ring between two support towers, standing in a concrete basin with a trench that keeps the whole ring visible.
