# Beyond the Edge: Space Lab

**Beyond the Edge: Space Lab** is a standalone Factorio: Space Age post-game mod. It adds one advanced orbital biotechnology building (<img src="docs/icons/bte-growth-facility.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> **Growth Facility**), one trace asteroid mineral (<img src="docs/icons/bte-flurolite.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> **Flurolite**), three post-game technologies (**Asteroid Stone Processing**, **Flurolite Processing**, and **Growth Facility**), and surface-condition overrides that let a space platform craft every planetary science pack in orbit.

### Demo video on YouTube (1.7 min)

[![Watch the Space Lab demo on YouTube](https://img.youtube.com/vi/JVCqwXjkNm8/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JVCqwXjkNm8)

The mod requires `base >= 2.1.0` and `space-age >= 2.1.0`. [Beyond the Edge: Warp Drive](../warp-drive/README.md) is optional.

Safe to add to an existing late-game save: the mod only adds new recipes, technologies, and a building. The one vanilla change removes the surface conditions from four science pack recipes, which only allows more, so existing factories keep running exactly as before.

## Overview

Once a Space Age factory reaches the Solar System Edge, planetary production is established, but space platforms still depend on planet-side rockets for stone, ammonia, lithium, fluorine, Gleba crops, eggs, and the four planetary science packs.

Space Lab turns a space platform into a self-sufficient orbital factory:

- **Complete asteroid extraction:** New Crusher and Cryogenic Plant recipes recover bulk <img src="docs/icons/stone.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Stone, <img src="docs/icons/ammonia.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Ammonia, <img src="docs/icons/bte-flurolite.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Flurolite, <img src="docs/icons/lithium-brine.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Lithium Brine, <img src="docs/icons/fluorine.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Fluorine, <img src="docs/icons/holmium-ore.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Holmium Ore, <img src="docs/icons/tungsten-ore.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Tungsten Ore, and trace <img src="docs/icons/uranium-ore.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Uranium Ore directly from asteroid chunks.
- **Orbital biotechnology:** The <img src="docs/icons/bte-growth-facility.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> **Growth Facility** cultivates <img src="docs/icons/yumako.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Yumako and <img src="docs/icons/jellynut.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Jellynut from seeds and incubates <img src="docs/icons/biter-egg.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Biter Eggs and <img src="docs/icons/pentapod-egg.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Pentapod Eggs in orbit while preserving products inside its output inventory.
- **Orbital science production:** Planetary surface conditions are removed from the <img src="docs/icons/agricultural-science-pack.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Agricultural, <img src="docs/icons/cryogenic-science-pack.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Cryogenic, <img src="docs/icons/electromagnetic-science-pack.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Electromagnetic, and <img src="docs/icons/metallurgic-science-pack.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Metallurgic Science Pack recipes, so standard machines can run the entire late-game science chain on a space platform.

## Asteroid Extraction & Cryogenic Chemistry

Vanilla asteroid crushing never produces <img src="docs/icons/stone.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Stone. Space Lab adds two crushing tiers for the three common asteroid chunks (<img src="docs/icons/metallic-asteroid-chunk.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Metallic, <img src="docs/icons/carbonic-asteroid-chunk.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Carbonic, and <img src="docs/icons/oxide-asteroid-chunk.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Oxide), plus a dedicated recipe for <img src="docs/icons/promethium-asteroid-chunk.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Promethium Asteroid Chunks (**Red Asteroids**):

- **Stone-Focused crushing** (**Asteroid Stone Processing**): Fast `2s` recipes that yield `4` <img src="docs/icons/stone.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Stone alongside the chunk's primary resource (<img src="docs/icons/iron-ore.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Iron / <img src="docs/icons/copper-ore.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Copper, <img src="docs/icons/carbon.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Carbon, or <img src="docs/icons/ice.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Ice).
- **Flurolite-Focused crushing** (**Flurolite Processing**): `3s` recipes that trade half the Stone (`2` <img src="docs/icons/stone.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Stone) for independent rolls of `5%` <img src="docs/icons/bte-flurolite.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Flurolite and `1%` <img src="docs/icons/uranium-ore.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Uranium Ore. (<img src="docs/icons/calcite.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Calcite remains on vanilla advanced Oxide crushing.)
- **Shattered Synthesis** (**Flurolite Processing**): Crushes <img src="docs/icons/promethium-asteroid-chunk.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Promethium Asteroid Chunks in `4s` into guaranteed <img src="docs/icons/tungsten-ore.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Tungsten Ore and <img src="docs/icons/stone.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Stone, plus `20%` <img src="docs/icons/holmium-ore.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Holmium Ore, `5%` <img src="docs/icons/bte-flurolite.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Flurolite, and `3%` <img src="docs/icons/uranium-ore.png" width="24" height="24" alt=""> Uranium Ore.

## Scientific Justification

- **Ammonia from Stone:** Carbonaceous asteroids contain nitrogen-bearing phyllosilicates. A Cryogenic Plant uses high-pressure and heat processing to extract ammonia directly from stone.
- **Stone from Crushing:** Vanilla asteroid processing never yields Stone, but mechanical shattering of any silicate or metallic body inherently produces mineral rubble regardless of composition. The Stone-Focused Crusher variants isolate that rubble without the finer sieving needed to detect Flurolite traces, which is why they are the cheaper, faster option.
- **Flurolite Chemistry:** Lithium and fluorine are geochemically linked in evolved crustal granites. Flurolite represents the trace rare-earth inclusions found within shattered planetary crusts, processed through cryogenic plant distillation. Red Asteroids carry the richest concentration, but trace inclusions also turn up in ordinary Metallic, Carbonic, and Oxide chunks; finding them requires the finer sieving of the Flurolite-Focused Crusher variants, at the cost of some Stone throughput. Calcite recovery is left untouched on the existing Oxide processing recipe, which already yields it in ample quantity.
- **Uranium Trace Recovery:** Uranium Ore appears as a rare heavy-element inclusion in the same advanced asteroid separation step that detects Flurolite. Common asteroid chunks only expose tiny recoverable traces, while Red Asteroids justify a higher recovery chance because their heavy-metal profile is already represented by Holmium and Tungsten outputs.
- **Growth Facility:** Rather than "stabilizing" life, the Growth Facility provides the precise environmental parameters — heat, light, nutrients, Bioflux enrichment, nitrogen atmosphere, calcite buffering, carbon feedstock, and high-energy water hydrolysis for oxygen supply — required to maintain planetary species in a low-gravity environment while keeping the high-stakes spoilage mechanics of Space Age intact. Yumako and Jellynut are cultivated from their corresponding seeds as a matched pair, exactly as they grow together on Gleba, while Biter and Pentapod eggs require separate incubation recipes so the platform's Bioflux, Nutrients, and egg chains stay self-sufficient.
