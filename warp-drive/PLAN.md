# Warp Drive Plan

## Release Validation

- Test a naturally moving platform through departure, reversal, arrival, and endpoint margins.
- Measure normal connection-distance movement and calibrate the scripted delta to confirm `100x` effective speed.
- Test powered support demand, partial brownouts, disconnected power, warp exit, and the 10-second re-entry lockout.
- Compare Practical Warp Drive bonus progress with ordinary lab progress, including quality, modules, beacons, force bonuses, productivity, and optional SPACELAB facilities.
- Test drive placement and duplicate refunds for players, robots, platform construction, and ghosts.
- Verify the expanded detector/warp status GUI layout, live values, automatic-engagement toggling, and close behavior.
- Verify actual asteroid removal during warp.
- Visually inspect animation order, emission, shadow, shift, collision footprint, and selection box.
- Connect both circuit-wire colors to nearby poles and verify attachment, sag, shadows, and that parallel wires do not cross.

## Deferred Features

- Dedicated activation and exit sounds.
- Bounded subspace-streak rendering.
- GUI research projections and connection names.
- Enable and validate optional SPACELAB Growth Facility amplification.
- Remote interfaces for third-party integrations.
