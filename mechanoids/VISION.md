# Beyond the Edge: Mechanoids — Guardians of the Ruins — vision

**The ruins are abandoned. Their guardians are not.**

Mechanoids is a standalone enemy mod for Factorio: Space Age. Ancient mechanical defenders make exploring ruins a reason to prepare weapons, armor, supplies, and a way out.

> Planning only. Standalone placement and rewards are still being designed. [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) preserves the combat design, implementation references, and open questions. The mod requires Factorio and Space Age, not Stargate or the campaign.

## A first home on Fulgora

The current proposal is to place standalone Mechanoids **only on Fulgora**, whose existing ruins give them a natural setting. The player encounters surviving security machines among the remains of an older civilization, rather than a gate campaign appearing on every planet.

This is a direction to investigate, not a finished encounter-generation specification. How frequently guarded areas appear, what exactly they protect, and whether clearance gives salvage or simply safer access still need design work. The mod must be worth encountering without requiring a gate, quest artifact, or homecoming objective.

## Recognizable machines, readable battles

The initial roster has two roles:

- **Skirmishers:** low, fast melee machines that pressure the player's movement.
- **Sentinels:** ranged defenders that cover approaches and reward careful positioning.

Heavier **Bulwarks** can follow once the first two produce enjoyable encounters. Difficulty should come from composition, terrain, and preparation, not fifteen copies of a fight with ever larger health bars.

Ordinary Factorio solutions remain valid: personal weapons, combat robots, fortifications, mines, artillery, vehicles, and cooperation. The machines defend their ruin rather than chasing an engineer indefinitely across the planet.

## A cleared ruin stays cleared

The starting design is a finite roster per defended site. Damaging a guardian and retreating should retain that damage; defeating it should leave lasting progress. Reloading, re-entering the area, or installing another family mod must not refill the encounter.

The exact standalone spawning policy remains open, but infinite reinforcements and factory-wide invasions are not the baseline. Peaceful and enemies-disabled configurations also need a deliberate policy before release.

## Guardians for a larger journey

[Homecoming Journey](../homecoming-journey/VISION.md) can ask this mod to defend authored ruins on all five planets. The campaign decides where those sites are, what their clearance unlocks, and which artifacts they contain. Mechanoids remains responsible for its enemies and their encounter behavior.

[Stargate](../stargate/VISION.md) stays independent: building experimental gates does not require fighting Mechanoids, and exploring guarded Fulgoran ruins does not require researching a gate.
