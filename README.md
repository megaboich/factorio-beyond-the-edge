S.I.O.S. (Singularity-Integrated Optimized Synthesis)
A late-game overhaul mod for Factorio: Space Age.
Overview
S.I.O.S. is a high-tier industrial expansion designed for the Space Age endgame. Once you have mastered the distinct challenges of Vulcan, Fulgora, Gleba, and Aquilo, this mod provides the final technological bridge: the ability to bring planetary-tier production into orbit.
By utilizing asteroids as a primary chemical feedstock and mastering orbital-scale biological cultivation, S.I.O.S. transforms your space platform into a Grand Unified Factory, capable of producing every science pack in the game without the need for constant planetary maintenance.
Design Philosophy
•	Additive & Compatible: This mod does not replace or overwrite any vanilla recipes. All content is gated behind new, high-tier research, ensuring full compatibility with existing late-game saves.
•	Logistics Mastery: The mod replaces "planetary dependency" with "orbital complexity." Production chains are energy-intensive and require advanced circuit-logic balancing.
•	Closed-Loop Economy: By extracting rare chemical inputs from asteroid debris, you turn your platform into a self-sustaining ecosystem.
Core Features
New Materials
•	Flurolite (Fluoro-Spodumene): A rare, trace mineral found as a byproduct of asteroid processing. It is the mandatory precursor for high-tier chemical synthesis.
New Buildings
•	Hydrothermal Processor: Extracts Nitrogen-based compounds (Ammonia) from Stone and Water.
•	Cryogenic Condenser: A high-power facility that balances the output of Lithium Brine and Fluorine Gas from Flurolite.
•	Orbital Bio-Lab: An advanced facility that allows for the growth of Gleba fruits and the incubation of Biter/Pentapod eggs in orbit.

Scientific Justification
•	Ammonia from Stone: Carbonaceous asteroids contain nitrogen-bearing phyllosilicates. The Hydrothermal Processor uses high-pressure/heat processing to "cook" ammonia directly from stone.
•	Flurolite Chemistry: Lithium and Fluorine are geochemically linked in evolved crustal granites. Flurolite represents the trace rare-earth inclusions found within shattered planetary crusts, processed via cryogenic distillation.
•	Orbital Bio-Lab: Rather than "stabilizing" life, the Bio-Lab provides the precise environmental parameters (heat, light, and nutrients) required to maintain planetary species in a low-gravity environment, keeping the high-stakes spoilage mechanics of Space Age intact.
The Endgame Challenge
S.I.O.S. is not a "shortcut." It is a test of your mastery over Factorio's logistical systems. Success requires balancing your energy grid to support 500MW+ synthesis machines and using circuit networks to optimize chemical yields. It is the final milestone for the factory that must grow.
